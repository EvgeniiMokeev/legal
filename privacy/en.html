Privacy Policy

DailyPriorities · Last updated: May 27, 2026

Overview

DailyPriorities is designed with privacy in mind. We do not collect, sell, or share your personal data. Your priorities and history stay on your device.

Data stored on your device

The following data is stored locally on your device and in your iCloud account (via App Group shared storage). We have no access to it:

  • Your daily priorities (task text and completion status)
  • Your history and streaks
  • App settings and preferences
  • AI-generated feedback saved for past days

AI Feedback (Premium subscribers only)

When you complete all three daily priorities, the text of your tasks is sent to our server (a Cloudflare Worker) to generate a short AI-powered review via OpenAI. This happens only if you have an active Premium subscription.

  • Only the text of your three tasks is transmitted — no device identifiers or account data.
  • The request is not logged or stored on our server. It is forwarded directly to OpenAI's API and the response is returned to your device.
  • OpenAI's data handling is governed by OpenAI's Privacy Policy.

Purchases and subscriptions

Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment information. Subscription status is verified via RevenueCat, which processes only anonymized entitlement data. RevenueCat's privacy policy is available at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Analytics and tracking

We do not use any analytics tools, crash reporters, or advertising SDKs. We do not track your behavior inside the app.

Children's privacy

DailyPriorities is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
